# One Entry, not a separate Sighting

"Saw 25, took 15" is one field report, not a collection plus a leftover. Entry already meant the log; it now carries both `seen` and `collected`. Either may be blank or zero. The season goal sums `collected` only.

We rejected a separate Sighting record and a status flag on today's collection-only Tree Entry. A status flag will leak spotted trees into totals. Two records for one standing-there moment will drift. The word stays Entry — no rename campaign for five weeks of use a year.
